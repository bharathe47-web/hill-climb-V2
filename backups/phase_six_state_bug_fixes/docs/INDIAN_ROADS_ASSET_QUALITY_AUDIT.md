# Indian Roads Asset Quality Audit & Replacement Register

**Project:** Hill Climb 2D — Indian Roads Mode  
**Document Owner:** Joel (Lead Indian Roads Specialist & Art Director)  
**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** Quality Audit & Production Replacements Staged  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This comprehensive audit evaluates the visual quality of the existing assets in the **Indian Roads** mode against AAA commercial 2D mobile standards. 
Every asset has been individually inspected for pixel resolution, alpha transparency, stylistic fidelity, and in-game appearance.

High-resolution, commercially licensed replacements have been acquired, processed into game-ready transparent sprites, and staged in `assets/indian_roads_replacements/` for verification prior to production rollout.
