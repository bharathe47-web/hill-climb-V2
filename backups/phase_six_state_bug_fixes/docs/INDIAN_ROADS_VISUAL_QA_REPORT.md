## 1. REMOVAL OF PINK PLACEHOLDER OBJECT (STEP 1B)

### Root Cause Analysis
- **File:** `assets/indian_locations/tamil_nadu/vegetation/shrub_tamil_bougainvillea_pink.png`.
- **Cause:** Generated by `scripts/generation/gen_tamil_nadu.py` lines 471–474 using 7 hardcoded magenta circles (`rgb(0.92, 0.15, 0.55)`).
- **Resolution:** Replaced with an authentic natural lush green tropical shrub texture (`160x130 px`) featuring multi-tone leafy gradients (deep forest green, moss jade, sunny leaf highlights), woody branch architecture, and zero neon circles or geometric blobs.
- **Verification:** Headless landmark tests and real in-engine gameplay screenshots confirm complete eradication of the bright pink circles across all Tamil Nadu routes.

## 3. LANDMARK COMPOSITION & ANCHORING (STEP 1D)

All 44 famous landmarks follow the 5-tier placement hierarchy:
1. `DRIVABLE_STRUCTURE` — Elevated driving surface (Kanyakumari Glass Bridge with $C^1$ smoothstep ramps).
2. `ROADSIDE_LANDMARK` — Roadside monuments placed directly beside the asphalt with grounded masonry foundations.
3. `BACKGROUND_LANDMARK` — Offshore or distant monuments with dedicated platform elevations (Vivekananda Rock, Thiruvalluvar pedestal).
4. `NATURAL_ATTRACTION` — Waterfalls and coastal panoramas.
5. `ROADSIDE_PROP` — Milestones, signage, dhabas.
