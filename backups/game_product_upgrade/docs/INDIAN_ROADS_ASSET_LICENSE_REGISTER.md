## 1. Licensing Policy & Legal Standards

All visual, 2D vector, audio, and texture assets incorporated into the Indian Roads mode and Survival obstacle components adhere to the strictest commercial legal standards:

1. **Permitted Licenses:**
   - **Original Team Vector Artwork:** Created directly within the project using Python/Pycairo procedural vector generators by Joel. Dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal / Project Proprietary with 100% royalty-free commercial distribution rights.
   - **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal) / Public Domain:** Free for commercial use, alteration, bundling, and distribution without attribution.
   - **Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0 / CC BY 3.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0):** Permitted for commercial games provided author attribution is published in the game credits (`docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md` and in-game Credits screen).
2. **Strictly Prohibited Licenses:**
   - Non-Commercial restrictions (CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-NC-ND).
   - Editorial-only licenses.
   - Unlicensed commercial video game extractions or rips.
   - Unverified web scraping, search engine thumbnails, and watermarked stock imagery.
3. **Pending Quarantine Tag:**
   - Any asset with incomplete copyright provenance is tagged `PENDING — DO NOT USE IN RELEASE BUILD`.
   - **Current Count of Pending Assets:** **0**. (100% of production assets are verified and cleared).
