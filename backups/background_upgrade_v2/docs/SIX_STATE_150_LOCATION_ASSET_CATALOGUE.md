## 1. Overview & Architectural Hierarchy

This catalogue documents **150 iconic Indian locations** across the six game states:
1. **Tamil Nadu** (Southern Coromandel & Coastal Ghats)
2. **Goa** (Konkan Coast & Western Ghats)
3. **Kerala** (Malabar Coast & Nilgiri Hills)
4. **Rajasthan** (Thar Desert & Aravalli Range)
5. **Gujarat** (Rann of Kutch & Saurashtra Coast)
6. **Telangana** (Deccan Plateau & Kakatiya Heartland)

### World Layer Definitions
- **`Layer 1: Roadside Foreground`** (Z-index: 10 to 15): Interactive milestones, toll gates, barricades, roadside shops, and food stalls.
- **`Layer 2: Roadside Landmark`** (Z-index: 5): Primary cultural monuments and waypoint landmarks positioned on roadside pedestals decoupled from signboards.
- **`Layer 3: Near Village / Urban Facade`** (Z-index: 0 to 2, Parallax 0.38): Architecture rows (Fontainhas Portuguese houses, Chettinad homes, mud bhungas, havelis).
- **`Layer 4: Midground Landscape`** (Z-index: -5, Parallax 0.22): Backwaters, salt flats, sand dunes, lakes, tea plantation contours.
- **`Layer 5: Distant Horizon`** (Z-index: -10, Parallax 0.12): Lush Western Ghats, Aravalli ridgelines, Deccan granite boulder outcrops.
- **`Layer 6: Far Sky`** (Z-index: -20, Parallax 0.04): Regional atmospheric sky gradients with soft celestial illumination.
