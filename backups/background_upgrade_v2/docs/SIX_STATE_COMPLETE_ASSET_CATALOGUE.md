## 1. Specification & Field Index (29 Attributes)

Every asset in this authoritative register is documented with exactly 29 verified technical and legal metadata attributes:
1. **Asset ID**: Unique persistent identifier (`LOC-ST-XX`)
2. **Famous Place Name**: Culturally and historically verified title
3. **State**: One of the six V1 states (Tamil Nadu, Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Telangana)
4. **District / Region**: Administrative district and local geographical sector
5. **Real Location & Coordinates**: Real-world latitude and longitude coordinates
6. **Cultural & Historic Significance**: Why the monument is celebrated worldwide
7. **Architectural Description**: Silhouette, materials, structural features, and elevation
8. **Asset Category**: `landmark`, `building`, `vegetation`, `prop`, `background`, `texture`
9. **Hierarchy & Priority**: `Tier 1 core waypoint`, `Tier 2 secondary milestone`, `Tier 3 optional scenery`
10. **Suggested Route Zone**: Route biome zone (Coast, Delta, Ghats, Desert, Plateau, etc.)
11. **Suggested Godot Scene**: Engine node scene mapping (`RoadsideLandmark.tscn`, `IndianRoadTerrainV2.tscn`, etc.)
12. **World Layer & Parallax**: Depth layer, Z-index (-15 to 15), and parallax scroll factor (0.04 to 1.0)
13. **Roadside Placement**: Placement style (Ground stone pedestal, midground elevation, horizon silhouette)
14. **Recommended Resolution**: Master asset resolution (512x512, 768x768, 1024x1024)
15. **Source Website**: Curated source portal (Wikimedia, Openverse, CraftPix, itch.io, Kenney, Original)
16. **Original Source URL**: Live permanent URL reference
17. **Creator / Copyright Holder**: Author, institution, or open-source contributor
18. **Licence**: Formal legal license designation
19. **Commercial Permission**: Explicit confirmation of commercial monetization rights
20. **Modification Permission**: Confirmation of derivative and resizing rights
21. **Attribution Requirement**: Mandatory attribution requirements and citation format
22. **Asset Origin**: `Original Game Vector Art`, `Curated Derivative Vector Artwork`, `Third-party CC Asset`
23. **Project File Status**: Presence on local disk in project repository
24. **Source Downloaded**: Asset data successfully acquired locally
25. **Asset Processed**: Transparent PNG cutout, color-grading, and normalization completed
26. **Godot Imported**: Recognized and imported by Godot resource pipeline (.import generated)
27. **Scene Referenced**: Wired into route registry, landmark database, or terrain spawner
28. **Gameplay Visible**: Verified appearing beside the blue player vehicle on screen
29. **Release Approval Status**: One of 9 formal lifecycle statuses: `VERIFIED SOURCE`, `DOWNLOADED`, `LICENCE VERIFIED`, `PROCESSING REQUIRED`, `READY FOR GODOT`, `INTEGRATED`, `VISIBLE IN GAMEPLAY`, `RELEASE APPROVED`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT AVAILABLE`
