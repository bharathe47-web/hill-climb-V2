## 1. Executive Summary & Visual Baseline Critique

Analysis of the initial visual gameplay screenshots revealed several critical visual defects that degraded the player experience:
1. **Flat Sky & Sea:** The Kanyakumari starting area lacked oceanic depth; sky and water were flat, monotonous blue strips with zero atmospheric perspective or wave motion.
2. **Disconnected Starting Monument:** `tamil_nadu_kanyakumari_triveni_sangam.png` was displayed as an unintegrated rectangular poster block hovering over the terrain rather than naturally embedded in a scrolling world.
3. **Severe Landmark Silhouette Defects:**
   - `tamil_nadu_madurai_meenakshi_temple.png` was a 240×320 flat black silhouette with zero representation of the world-famous colorful sculpted gopuram.
   - `tamil_nadu_thiruvalluvar_statue.png` was a 200×300 basic line outline with zero stone texture, missing pedestal clarity, and poor contrast.
   - `tamil_nadu_chennai_marina_lighthouse.png` was an untextured black silhouette lacking the modernist triangular red-and-white bands.
4. **Signboard / Label Overlap:** The milestone and location title labels in `Landmark.tscn` directly overlapped the center of the monument artwork (`position = Vector2(0, -210)` over an asset centered at `y = -110`).
5. **Inappropriate Winter Trees:** Tamil Nadu tropical road stretches featured white, leafless winter trees that broke regional realism.
6. **Primitive Highway Ground:** The asphalt was a single thin polygon line with a flat brown dirt fill, lacking road wear, shoulder aggregate, and edge delineation.
7. **Repetitive Low-Detail Props:** Roadside shops, toll booths, and barriers were tiny (under 50px) low-color placeholders.

All of these issues have been addressed in **Visual Upgrade v2**.
