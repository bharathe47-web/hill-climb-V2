## 1. Executive Summary

This comprehensive audit investigates visual, physical, and functional defects observed across all six Indian Roads states (Tamil Nadu, Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Telangana), with particular focus on vehicle wheel alignment during nitro acceleration, landmark ground positioning, and the Kanyakumari Glass Bridge playable ocean structure.
