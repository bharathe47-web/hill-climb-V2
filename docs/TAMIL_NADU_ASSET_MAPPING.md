## 3. Geographical Alignment & Regional Detour Strategy

- **Off-Road Detour Handling:** Certain iconic attractions (such as Thirparappu Waterfalls in Kanyakumari, Courtallam Falls in Tirunelveli, and Samanar Hills near Madurai/Melur) are geographically located off the direct NH44/NH45 trunk route. In accordance with design guidelines, these are implemented as **scenic roadside detours / natural background attractions** with advance tourism signboards, rather than appearing as artificial obstructions placed on the asphalt carriageway.
- **Urban Centers & Royal Palaces:** Madurai showcases both Meenakshi Amman Temple Rajagopuram ($500\text{ m}$) and Thirumalai Nayak Palace ($540\text{ m}$) with distinct architectural silhouettes—the palace retains its iconic Italianate stucco arches and colossal cylindrical pillars.
