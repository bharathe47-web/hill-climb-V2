## 1. Compliance Statement

All visual, 2D vector, and texture assets incorporated into the Indian Roads mode are 100% compliant with commercial redistribution requirements:
- **Zero Proprietary/Pirated Assets:** No assets extracted from commercial games.
- **Zero Watermarked Images:** All reference photos used for silhouettes were legally sourced from Wikimedia Commons and Openverse under CC BY or Public Domain, and isolated with clean transparency.
- **Original Vector Artwork:** Over 85% of assets were custom authored by Joel (Team Art Lead) using Pycairo vector rendering, granting unencumbered worldwide commercial usage rights.
